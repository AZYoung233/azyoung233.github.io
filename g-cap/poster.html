G-Cap: A Game Character Caption Generator

Code & Data
1 Motivation

LVLMs excel at captioning real-world images, yet surreal, highly stylized virtual worlds (games & anime) remain largely unexplored — despite real industrial value in content retrieval, skin recommendation & design, and review.

Case study. Even SOTA GPT-5.1 captions the real world well, but fails to replicate that success on virtual-world game characters — missing fine-grained, hierarchical clothing detail.
Q1. How to evaluate virtual-world captioning?  Q2. How to close the open–closed-source gap?

3 Graph-F1 Metric ★ KEY

NLG metrics & VLM-as-judge are too coarse for dense captions. Graph-F1 parses captions into semantic graphs and scores them by two-stage soft subgraph matching — parent nodes aligned by cosine similarity, then one-hop subgraphs scored by a reranker. Embedding + reranker run locally (~10 GB GPU); only triplet parsing uses an LLM.

Pipeline. Captions → semantic graphs → asymmetric precision (hallucination check) and recall (coverage check); Graph-F1 is their harmonic mean.
Best human alignment: Graph-F1 0.79  vs  GPT-5 Judge 0.60 · CAPTURE 0.59 · BERTScore 0.57.
4 GC-148K Dataset

A 148K image–caption corpus that bridges the gap, built via a Deconstruct–Recombine–Generate pipeline.

Pipeline. A Fashion Simulator recombines a 560K-instance garment library via Nano Banana.
  • GC-Real (78K) — web images, DINOv3-deduplicated, densely captioned by Gemini 2.5 Pro.
  • GC-Syn (70K) — synthesized multi-layer clothing hierarchies.

6 Analysis: Density vs. Length

Gains come from structural coverage, not longer text: GPT-5.1 is verbose (>500 words) yet low-density (0.49), while Gemini-family models & G-Cap maximize information per word.

Bubble = Graph-F1. Future captioners should jointly maximize density and coverage rather than chase length alone.
7 Takeaways ★
  • Task. Game Character Captioning — LVLM captioning for virtual worlds.
  • Benchmark. GC-Bench (186 images) + Graph-F1, the metric best aligned with humans.
  • Data. GC-148K via a Fashion-Simulator synthesis pipeline.
  • Result. G-Cap rivals advanced closed-source LVLMs at lower cost.
In one line: a benchmark, a metric & an open recipe bringing industrial-grade game-character captioning within reach.
